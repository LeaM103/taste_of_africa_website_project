Discover African Recipes

Explore traditional African dishes, discover their cultural backgrounds and learn how to prepare them at home.

1. Jollof Rice

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish prepared with rice, tomatoes, peppers, onions and spices. It is commonly served during celebrations and family gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • 2 pieces tomatoes
  • 1 piece red bell pepper
  • 1 piece onion
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt and spices
View cooking instructions
  1. Blend tomatoes, peppers and onions.
  2. Heat oil and cook the tomato paste.
  3. Add the blended mixture and spices.
  4. Add washed rice and water.
  5. Cover and cook until the rice is soft.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy!

2. Injera

Injera is a traditional Ethiopian flatbread made from fermented teff flour. It is usually served with different stews and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Teff flour
  • Water
  • A small amount of salt
View cooking instructions
  1. Mix teff flour and water.
  2. Allow the mixture to ferment.
  3. Heat a non-stick pan.
  4. Pour in a thin layer of batter.
  5. Cover and cook until the surface sets.
  6. Serve with your favourite stew.

3. Moroccan Tagine

Tagine is a North African dish named after the traditional cooking pot. It is commonly prepared with vegetables, meat and aromatic spices.

Ingredients

  • Chicken or vegetables
  • Onions
  • Carrots and potatoes
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic
  • Ginger, cumin and other spices
View cooking instructions
  1. Heat olive oil in a cooking pot.
  2. Add onions and garlic.
  3. Add your chicken or vegetables.
  4. Add spices and a little water.
  5. Cover and cook slowly until tender.
  6. Serve warm.

4. Isombe

Isombe is a popular Rwandan dish made from cassava leaves. It is often cooked with groundnuts and served with other traditional foods.

Ingredients

  • Crushed cassava leaves
  • Groundnut paste
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt
View cooking instructions
  1. Wash and prepare the cassava leaves.
  2. Cook the leaves until tender.
  3. Add onions and garlic.
  4. Mix in groundnut paste.
  5. Simmer until everything is well cooked.
  6. Serve with your preferred side dish.
